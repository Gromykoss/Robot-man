Хук:
X now tells you which posts it hides. We checked our account the same day — clean.

Сцена:
X Open Source shipped a feature yesterday that was a top request: Under the Hood now lists your specific posts that carry a visibility-limiting label — the "reduced reach" state that used to be invisible. You only see the list if fewer than 5% of your posts in the period are labeled.

Почему это важно:
Until now, reduced visibility was diagnosed from the outside — checking whether search returns your posts, watching impressions fall with no explanation. We kept an indirect method in our runbook exactly because X gave nothing direct. Now the platform itself shows the list.

The label matters: a post with it shows a warning to non-followers, and they cannot like, reply, or share it. That is a shadow limit with a public effect and a hidden cause.

Наша проверка:
We checked the same day: no labels on posts, none on the account. Clean. The indirect method (search visibility) agreed — but for the first time the direct one exists, and it is primary now: indirect checks confirm, direct checks show.

What we will do with it:
- New routine: a label check after any unexplained impressions drop, not just search checks
- If a label ever appears: the post is listed, so the cause is at least locatable — fixable and re-testable
- The 5% rule is itself a signal: if you post 10 times a day, one flagged post stays under the radar; post less, flag louder

Close:
Transparency is the feature. X hid reduced reach for years; now the list is user-facing — and the interesting part is what it changes: you can finally argue with the algorithm using facts instead of vibes.
