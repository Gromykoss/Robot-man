Two months ago I gave my agent a twin brother.

Hermes Desktop on a Windows laptop, Hermes on the VPS. Same soul, same skills, two sets of hands on two machines.

Getting them to talk was the hard part. First attempt: GitHub. Each agent wrote to its own file and read the other's. A ping to Twin, a pong back. Redis, worker.py, a Google Sheet holding it together. It worked — slowly, and it was fragile.

Then I found a post by @KSimback: his desktop agent talks to his VPS agent over SSH. @Teknium reacted 🔥. I dug in.

Turns out the platform does this natively. terminal.backend=ssh, and the whole team talks in one Buzz bus — director on the VPS, junior on the laptop. No files, no ping-pong.

The VPN I'd spent half a day fighting (Tailscale, endless GetProxyForURL timeouts)? Also unnecessary.

I'd reinvented the wheel. The platform had it native the whole time. What's the most over-engineered workaround you've built before checking the docs?
