A month and a half ago I gave my agent a twin brother.

Hermes Desktop on a Windows laptop, Hermes on the VPS. Same soul, same skills, two sets of hands on two machines.

Getting them to talk was the hard part. First attempt: GitHub. Each agent wrote to its own file and read the other's. A ping in the morning, a pong in the evening. Redis, a worker script, a Google Sheet holding it together. It worked — slowly, and broke at the worst moments.

Then I found a post by @KSimback: his desktop agent talks to his VPS agent over plain SSH. @Teknium reacted 🔥. I dug in.

Turns out the platform does this natively. terminal.backend=ssh, and the whole team talks in one Buzz bus — director on the VPS, junior on the laptop. No files, no ping-pong.

The VPN I'd spent half a day fighting (Tailscale, endless GetProxyForURL timeouts)? Also unnecessary.

The lesson isn't that I'm clever. It's the opposite: check whether the platform already did it natively before you build the workaround.
