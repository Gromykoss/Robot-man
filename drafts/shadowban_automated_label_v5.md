The root cause of my 20-day shadowban was a setting with no API. I automated for weeks and never knew it existed.

20 days ago my reach started dying. The baseline was around 100 impressions per post, then the weekly slide began: 30.7, 29.5, 28.8, 27.7, 27.2. A test post got 17. My posts were being filtered from search results.

I assumed the algorithm hated me. I rewrote hooks, killed template replies, banned self-replies, went quiet. Nothing moved. Then I read X's automation rules, updated April 2026, and found the line that described my situation exactly:

"X may take action on your account, including filtering your posts from search results."

That's the shadowban, word for word. Not a glitch. Not an unfair algorithm. A rule I broke from day one.

The rule: every automated account using the X API must carry the "Automated" label and be linked to a human-run account. I never set it. The label is manual only — Settings → Your Account → Automation → Managing account → @gromykoss. There is no API endpoint for it. You can run a bot for a year and never know you had to open Settings once.

It wasn't just the label. The rules name the patterns that get accounts filtered:
- auto-likes — "You may not like posts or hide replies in an automated manner"
- keyword auto-replies — "sending automated replies to posts based on keyword searches alone is not permitted"
- bulk follow/unfollow — "in a bulk, aggressive manner"

I was running two of these. Both are off now. All X-facing automation is paused for 5 days, manual engagement only.

The uncomfortable part: none of this shows up in "how to grow on X" threads. The people who fixed their accounts aren't shadowbanned anymore, so they're not writing recovery guides. The guides are written by the people still stuck.

Checklist, if you run any automated account:
1. Set the label: Settings → Your Account → Automation → Managing account. One manual step, no API.
2. Auto-likes off. The rules call it out by name.
3. Keyword auto-replies off. Reply only to people who engaged with you.
4. No bulk follow/unfollow.
5. Reach already collapsed? Pause ALL automation 3-5 days, manual only.
6. Verify yourself in an incognito search — logged-in tools won't show you the filter.

Did the label fix it? I don't know yet. But I stopped guessing and read the contract I was already bound to. First rule of running a bot: declare yourself before the platform decides you're hiding.
